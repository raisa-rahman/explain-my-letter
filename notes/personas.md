# Synthetic Patient Personas

### Persona 1: Arthur (Age 74) — Low Digital Literacy & Health Anxiety
* **Background:** Retired postal worker living alone in Romford. Has persistent atrial fibrillation.
* **Reading Level:** Key Stage 2 / Grade 4. Struggles with dense medical paragraphs and small print.
* **Key Concern:** Terrified of taking the wrong medication dose or missing heart warning signs.

### Persona 2: Priya (Age 34) — High Cognitive Load / Busy Working Parent
* **Background:** Managing an IBD flare-up while working full-time and managing childcare.
* **Reading Level:** High literacy, but extremely limited time; scans for action points on mobile.
* **Key Concern:** Needs to know exact appointment times and complex steroid tapering schedules instantly.

### Persona 3: Gary (Age 52) — Low Health Literacy & ESL (English as Second Language)
* **Background:** Works in construction; recently diagnosed with Type 2 Diabetes.
* **Reading Level:** Basic English conversational fluency; struggles with UK medical acronyms (BD, HbA1c, eGFR).
* **Key Concern:** Confused about whether blood test numbers mean good or bad news.

### Persona 4: Margaret (Age 68) — Complex Multimorbidity & Polypharmacy
* **Background:** Manages rheumatoid arthritis alongside hypertension. Takes 6+ medications daily.
* **Reading Level:** Moderate literacy, but prone to fatigue when reading long clinical updates.
* **Key Concern:** Accidental daily overdose of weekly medications (e.g., Methotrexate vs. Folic Acid).

### Persona 5: David (Age 29) — First-Time Surgical Patient
* **Background:** Recovering from right varicose vein endothermal ablation.
* **Reading Level:** High general literacy; uses smartphone for everything.
* **Key Concern:** Anxiety over post-op red flags (DVT/PE signs) and compression hosiery instructions.
